Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers to common cubic yard and construction material volume questions.

How do you calculate cubic yards?
Cubic Yards = (Length in feet × Width in feet × Depth in feet) ÷ 27. If depth is in inches, divide inches by 12 first, or multiply Length (ft) × Width (ft) × Depth (in) and divide by 324.
How many yards in a ton?
Because weight depends on material density: for crushed stone/gravel (1.4 tons/yd³), 1 ton ≈ 0.71 cubic yards; for topsoil (1.1 tons/yd³), 1 ton ≈ 0.91 cubic yards; for ready-mix concrete (2.03 tons/yd³), 1 ton ≈ 0.49 cubic yards.
How much does a yard of concrete weigh?
Standard poured ready-mix concrete weighs approximately 4,050 lbs (2.03 short tons) per cubic yard.
How many bags of concrete make a cubic yard?
It takes forty-five (45) 80 lb bags, sixty (60) 60 lb bags, or ninety (90) 40 lb bags of standard concrete mix to yield 1 cubic yard.
Can a pickup truck haul a cubic yard of gravel?
No. 1 cubic yard of crushed gravel weighs approximately 2,800 lbs, which exceeds the standard 1,500–1,800 lb payload limit of a standard 1/2-ton pickup (such as an F-150). It requires 2 trips or a heavier 1-ton truck.
